> **Why this profile matters.** This is the bar Learnish's *digital* layer is measured against. Khan Academy Kids is **free and excellent**, which sets the price anchor for "a learning app." Duolingo defines the **habit mechanic** (streaks, gamification) Learnish needs to match. Together they tell us: the app alone is not the business — it's the top of the funnel. The paid value must come from kits + workshops + local trust.

## Positioning & how parents use it

**Khan Academy Kids** is the trusted, free, no-ads early-learning app — the "responsible parent's" pick. **Duolingo** is the habit machine that proves kids (and adults) will return daily for a streak and a cartoon owl. Both are **digital-only, global, self-serve**.

Neither delivers a physical product, an in-person experience, or a locally-rooted community. Both are English/global-first, with thin Uzbek-language depth.

---

## Strengths (honest)

- **Free / cheap and high quality** — hard to beat on digital value; Khan is genuinely free and ad-free.
- **Brand trust** — Khan Academy carries strong "safe and educational" credibility globally.
- **Engagement design** — Duolingo's streak/gamification loop is the gold standard for retention.
- **Zero marginal cost** — they scale globally without local operations.

## Weaknesses / the wedge for Learnish

- **No local roots** — content, examples, and language are global/English-first; weak Uzbek-language and culturally-local material. Learnish is built **for Uzbek/Russian kids in Central Asia**.
- **Screen-only** — no kits in hand, no weekend workshop, no friends in a room. They cannot build the in-person trust + community that is Learnish's moat.
- **Narrow scope each** — Khan Kids skews young (2–8); Duolingo is mostly languages. Learnish spans **5–17 across multiple subjects** (entertainment → math → languages → more) **plus** physical + in-person.
- **No parent-community or belonging** — no local events, no ambassador network, no "Club."

---

## Competitive implications for Learnish

**Accept the free app as table stakes, not the product.**
- The free Learnish app + courses should be **generous** (it fuels the north-star, signups), explicitly *not* the thing you charge for. (See OQ-005.)
- **Borrow the habit mechanic** — Learnish needs its own daily ritual / streak / character ("Nish" the bee, D-013) to match Duolingo's retention. (See OQ-004.)
- **Win on what they can't do:** local-language depth, physical kits, weekend workshops, and real community. The pitch isn't "a better learning app" — it's "the app *plus* the kit *plus* the workshop *plus* the local club."
- **Localization is the moat at the digital layer** — Uzbek-first content (D-011) is a real differentiator vs. global apps.

**The trap question for a parent:** *"Can the app teach your child to build something with their hands — or introduce them to a friend this weekend?"*
