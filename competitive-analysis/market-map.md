# Market Map

The Learnish competitive landscape, on two axes: **attention** (child's time) and **category** (parent's spend).

> **Status:** Phase 5 of the [migration](../docs/migration-tracker.md). Named local players are the key gap — fill the "Local" rows as research lands.

---

## Axis 1 — Attention (competing for the child's time)

| Player | What it is | Why kids pick it | Learnish wedge |
|--------|-----------|------------------|----------------|
| YouTube / YouTube Kids | Free ad-supported video | Infinite, instant, free | Passive + trust-anxious → "learn, don't scroll" + safe (see [profile](../competitor-profiles/youtube-kids.md)) |
| TikTok / Instagram Reels | Short-form social video | Social, addictive | Not safe/age-appropriate for kids; not learning |
| Netflix / streaming | Premium video | Polished entertainment | Passive; no skill, output, or community |
| Roblox / mobile games | Social gaming | Play + friends online | Online-only; Learnish offers *real* making + *real* friends |

**How Learnish uses this axis:** it frames the *problem* and the emotional tension. Parents feel guilty about screen time and anxious about safety — Learnish sells the guilt-free, safe alternative.

---

## Axis 2 — Category (competing for the parent's learning spend)

| Sub-category | Players | Why parents pick it | Failure mode locally |
|--------------|---------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Free learning apps** | Khan Academy Kids, Duolingo / Duolingo ABC | Free, trusted, structured | Global/English-first, screen-only, no local roots ([profile](../competitor-profiles/khan-academy-kids.md)) |
| **STEAM kits (subscription)** | KiwiCo, MEL Science | Hands-on, curated, screen-free | Slow/costly shipping + customs into UZ, pricey, English-first ([profile](../competitor-profiles/steam-subscription-boxes.md)) |
| **In-person classes** | Local tutoring centers / kids clubs, Outschool | Local trust, real teachers, community | Fragmented, offline-only, single-subject, unbranded ([profile](../competitor-profiles/local-tutoring-and-clubs.md)) |
| **À-la-carte STEAM toys** | Uzum + local marketplaces, toy shops | Cheap, immediate, local | One-off; no curriculum, no progression, no community |
| **Do nothing** | Free YouTube + no structured learning | Zero cost/effort | The real default — and the real competitor |

## Positioning map (the moat)

Axes: **Local in-person trust / community** (low → high) vs **Structured learning value** (low → high)

```
              High structured learning value
                          │
   Khan / Duolingo        │        LEARNISH
   KiwiCo / MEL           │   (digital + kit + workshop
   (global, no local)     │    + local Club = both axes)
                          │
  Low local ───────────────────────── High local
  trust                   │                  trust
                          │
   YouTube / TikTok       │   Local tutoring centers
   (attention, no         │   & kids clubs
    learning)             │   (local, but unstructured/
                          │    fragmented/offline-only)
                          │
              Low structured learning value
```

**Learnish is the only player aiming for the top-right** — high learning value *and* high local trust — by combining a generous digital layer, a local kit operation, and in-person workshops under one safe, trusted Club brand.

## Local-commerce context (reused from archived Own research)

Relevant to the **STEAM toys store** and physical kit fulfillment. Source: [`_archive/own-fork/competitor-profiles/uzbekistan-landscape.md`](../_archive/own-fork/competitor-profiles/uzbekistan-landscape.md) — treat figures as directional, verify before quoting.

- **Where parents buy toys today:** Uzum (local marketplace unicorn), Wildberries, Ozon, AliExpress — Uzum is the main local à-la-carte STEAM-toy channel and a watch-list competitor for the store.
- **Payments:** UZCARD + HUMO cards; Click + Payme gateways; **BNPL** (Uzum Nasiya, Alif) is a real conversion lever — kits/Club should support it.
- **Logistics:** local delivery (Uzum Tezkor, Yandex, BTS) — a **local** kit operation beats global mail-order on speed/cost/customs (the KiwiCo/MEL weakness).
- **Behavior:** Telegram is the default first storefront/channel; three-language reality (Uzbek Latin + Russian + some English) — matches the [language matrix](../brand-voice/language-matrix.md).
- **Regulation:** rules increasingly favor locally-registered operators — structural tailwind for a local player.
