## Signal types (Learnish)

- **Attention shift** — a screen platform adds kids' "learning" shelves, or kids' content regulation tightens.
- **Global EdTech localizing** — Khan/Duolingo adding Uzbek content; a global kit (KiwiCo/MEL) opening local distribution.
- **Local entrant scaling** — a tutoring-center chain franchising or launching an app; a local STEAM-toy subscription.
- **Pricing/packaging change** — by any category competitor (resets the WTP anchor).
- **Partnership / distribution move** — venues, schools, marketplaces, payment players bundling kids' learning.
- **Parent behavior shift** — changes in screen-time attitudes, willingness to pay, channel preference.

## Rules

- Log a signal only if it would change a decision (positioning, pricing, channel, or roadmap).
- High-impact signals → open or update an item in the [open questions register](../docs/playbook/open-questions-register.md).
- Review monthly in Growth Ops; the highest-priority watch-list lives per-competitor in [`../competitor-profiles/`](../competitor-profiles/).
