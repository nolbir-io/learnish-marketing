# competitive-analysis

The deep view of who Learnish competes with — for the **child's attention** and for the **parent's learning spend** — in Uzbekistan / Central Asia.

> **Status:** Phase 5 (Competitive intelligence) of the [migration](../docs/migration-tracker.md), rewritten for Learnish.

## Purpose

Build and maintain a market map: attention competitors, category competitors, and the "do nothing" substitutes parents fall back on. This is the research layer; [`competitive-brief/`](../competitive-brief/) is the distilled, sales-ready output, and [`competitor-profiles/`](../competitor-profiles/) holds the per-competitor deep dives.

## The two-axis frame

- **Attention** — who competes for the *child's time*: YouTube/YouTube Kids, TikTok, Instagram, Netflix, Roblox.
- **Category** — who competes for the *parent's spend*: learning apps (Khan Academy Kids, Duolingo), STEAM kits (KiwiCo, MEL Science), in-person (local tutoring centers & kids clubs, Outschool), à-la-carte STEAM toys (Uzum / local marketplaces).

## Key questions to answer

- For attention: what does the child default to, and what guilt/anxiety does that create for the parent?
- For category: what does the parent buy today (app, box, class, toy), at what price, and why?
- What's the "do nothing" alternative — free YouTube + nothing structured?
- For each: positioning, pricing, traction, where they're strong, where they're soft locally.
- Which competitor moves should Learnish react to vs. ignore?
