## Telegram (ongoing, per workshop)

Telegram isn't on a fixed weekly slot — it runs around each confirmed workshop: **invite (T-7 days) → reminder (T-1) → recap + next-session invite + referral ask (T+1)**. Detail/templates live in `social/telegram/` (add as workshops are scheduled).

## Cadence

- **Instagram:** 3×/week (Mon/Wed/Fri).
- **LinkedIn:** 1×/week (Tuesdays).
- **Telegram:** event-driven around each workshop.

All posts in this batch support **Phase 1 — First 10 trusted families** (`../../campaigns/phase-1-brief.md`). Goal: fill free weekend workshops, drive free family-account signups (north-star), grow the local Telegram + Instagram following, and warm an investor/partner pipeline on LinkedIn.
