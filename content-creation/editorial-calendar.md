# Editorial Calendar

Monthly publishing rhythm, tied to the **workshop schedule**. Each workshop is a content event: it generates a reel, social posts, a Telegram recap, and an email. Everything supports the [campaign briefs](../campaigns/) and the workshop flywheel.

## Content mix targets

- **40% trust-building** — safety (reviewed, age-appropriate content), vetted teachers, founding-family stories, parents-in-the-room, real session proof.
- **30% workshop demand** — upcoming weekend workshops, "first one's free", what kids build, district-specific invites.
- **20% education / value** — STEAM-at-home ideas, what good screen time looks like, age-appropriate project tips (genuinely useful, not salesy).
- **10% Club + product** — Digital/Full Club value (intro pricing, being validated), STEAM store, app/course updates as they ship.

## Channel cadence

| Channel | Cadence | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Telegram** | Ongoing (invites + recaps around each workshop) | Primary parent comms + per-district community |
| **Instagram** | 3×/week (Mon/Wed/Fri) | Local awareness + real session proof |
| **LinkedIn** | 1×/week (Tue) | Investors / partners / hiring (not parents) |
| **Email** | Triggered + light weekly | Free account → Club activation; post-workshop follow-up |

## Repurposing rule (one workshop → many pieces)

Each weekend workshop should generate at least:

- **1 Instagram reel** (kids building, parent reaction) `[PROOF — real media, with consent]`
- **2–3 social adaptations** (carousel of what was built, a parent quote card, a "next workshop" invite)
- **1 Telegram recap** to the district group + next-session invite
- **1 email** to attendees (free account nudge / no-pressure Club intro)
- **1 founding-family story** when a family is willing to be featured (Phase 2+)
