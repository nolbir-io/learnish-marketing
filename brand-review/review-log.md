## Usage

- Log every reviewed item, even if rejected.
- Note the **audience** and **language(s)** so we can spot patterns (e.g. kid-facing copy too hyped, RU localization weak).
- Flag any **Safety & trust** or **Claim accuracy** zeros prominently — these are the dimensions that protect the brand promise.
- Each month, review recurring issues and feed corrections back into [`brand-voice/voice-guide.md`](../brand-voice/voice-guide.md) and [`brand-voice/language-matrix.md`](../brand-voice/language-matrix.md). Record any standard change in the [open questions register](../docs/playbook/open-questions-register.md).
