# brand-review

Process and rubric for checking that any piece of Learnish content is **safe, on-voice, and on-message** before it ships — to parents and to kids.

## Purpose

Catch off-brand language, off-message claims, weak localization, and anything that undermines the "safe and trusted" promise *before* content goes out. This folder owns the *check*; [`brand-voice/`](../brand-voice/) owns the *standard* it checks against. Because Learnish markets things for children and makes money from families, safety and claim accuracy are non-negotiable gates, not nice-to-haves.

## Files

- [`review-rubric.md`](review-rubric.md) — the scored checklist (safety, voice, dual-audience fit, claims, localization, CTA, age-appropriateness).
- [`review-log.md`](review-log.md) — decision history per asset, with audience and language tracked for pattern-spotting.

## Who reviews what

- **Marketing lead** — voice and message consistency.
- **Founder / DRI** — anything touching child safety, data, or money (claims and pricing).
- **Native UZ/RU reviewer** — localization quality, ideally a parent for parent-facing copy.

See [governance RACI](../docs/playbook/governance-raci.md) for decision rights and the [operating cadence](../docs/playbook/operating-cadence.md) for when reviews happen.

## How it flows

1. Author drafts using the [voice guide](../brand-voice/voice-guide.md) and [language matrix](../brand-voice/language-matrix.md).
2. Run the [rubric](review-rubric.md); minimum 13/16, with hard gates on safety and claim accuracy.
3. Record the outcome in the [review log](review-log.md).
4. Monthly, roll recurring issues back into the voice/language docs.
