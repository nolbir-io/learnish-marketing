# 6-Month Marketing Roadmap

Execution plan for standing up the Learnish community-led marketing engine — the workshop → signup → Club flywheel — and making it repeatable before aggressive scale.

## Planning Metadata

- **Plan owner:** Marketing Lead
- **Time horizon:** 6 months (extendable to 12 — note next-cycle brief in Month 06)
- **Review cadence:** Monthly
- **North-star:** Family account signups
- **Primary objective:** Prove a repeatable workshop→signup→Club motion in Tashkent before opening new nodes/regions.

## Month Sequence

- [Month 01](month-01.md) — Align ownership, definitions, baseline metrics, and the first workshop/venue.
- [Month 02](month-02.md) — Run the first lighthouse workshops and capture the first family signups.
- [Month 03](month-03.md) — Convert the founding cohort to Full Club and tighten signup→paid conversion.
- [Month 04](month-04.md) — Make workshops repeatable across venues with ambassadors running them.
- [Month 05](month-05.md) — Systematize the referral loop, Digital Club funnel, content, and SEO.
- [Month 06](month-06.md) — Consolidate learnings, review cohort retention, and plan the next cycle.

## Mapping to playbook phases

| Months | Playbook phase | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Phase 0 → Phase 1 prep | Foundation, offer, first venue |
| 02–03 | Phase 1 — First 10 families | Founding cohort via lighthouse workshops |
| 04–05 | Phase 2 — First 100 families | Repeatable, ambassador-run nodes + loops |
| 06 | Phase 2 → Phase 3 prep | Consolidate, retention, next-cycle brief |

## Guardrails

- Do not add new channels without an owner, a hypothesis, and a budget envelope.
- No content ships without review against the brand/safety rubric and KPI instrumentation.
- No workshop runs without a vetted teacher, a confirmed venue, and signup capture in place.
- Every month closes with documented decisions and updates to the playbook.
