# Month 01 — Align and Baseline

- **Owner:** Marketing Lead
- **Theme:** Alignment, instrumentation, and first-workshop readiness

## Milestones

1. Finalize RACI, operating cadence, and the open-questions register.
2. Define the north-star (family signups) and the full funnel: visit → free signup → activated family → Digital Club → Full Club.
3. Lock the Club offer and the first-workshop offer (entry price, included kit/seat) from `pricing/monetization-model.md`.
4. Line up the first venue, first vetted teacher, and first student ambassador; pick a workshop date.
5. Launch the weekly reporting template with baseline values.

## Dependencies

- Founder/CEO decisions on scope, budget, and the definition of an "active family."
- Named owners for community/workshop, campaign, content, and analytics.
- A bookable venue and at least one vetted teacher.

## Success Criteria

- Governance docs published and adopted in recurring meetings.
- Baseline KPI values captured for all core funnel stages.
- First workshop scheduled with venue, teacher, and signup capture confirmed.
- Top 5 unresolved assumptions have owners and due dates.
