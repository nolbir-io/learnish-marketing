# Month 06 — Consolidate and Plan Next Cycle

- **Owner:** Founder/CEO and Marketing Lead
- **Theme:** Learning consolidation and next-cycle readiness

## Milestones

1. Run a full 6-month retrospective with KPI, workshop P&L, and experiment outcomes.
2. Review the first cohort's retention (kit-shipment moment, workshop attendance, app habit).
3. Update playbook phase assumptions and the Club offer based on evidence.
4. Publish the next planning brief — including readiness to open the first regional/multi-venue node (Phase 3 prep).

## Dependencies

- Complete logs for workshops, content reviews, KPI trends, and experiments.
- Leadership alignment on the next strategic milestone (toward 1,000 families).
- Early retention data on the founding and early-adopter cohorts.

## Success Criteria

- Decisions and lessons documented and actionable.
- Cohort retention measured, with the biggest churn risk named and owned.
- Updated phase priorities agreed and communicated.
- Next planning cycle starts with fewer unresolved assumptions.
