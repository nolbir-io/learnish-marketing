# Learnish Playbook — The Road to 1,000 Families

A go-to-market playbook for building a safe, trusted kids' learning & development platform (ages 5–17) in Uzbekistan / Central Asia, organized by milestone phase. Read in order.

> **Status:** Learnish GTM spine (Phase 2 of the [migration](../migration-tracker.md)). Built from the agreed Phase 0 brief and the [monetization model](../../pricing/monetization-model.md). Numbers and timelines are working defaults until validated.

## What Learnish is (the one-paragraph version)

Learnish makes everyday learning exciting for kids 5–17 — through a freemium app and courses (languages, STEAM, personal finance), a STEAM toys store, and in-person weekend workshops. We sell to **parents**; the kids are the end users. The whole thing is framed as the **Learnish Club** (membership + community). The **north-star metric is family account signups**, and the primary call to action is *download the app / create a family account*.

## The growth engine — the workshop flywheel

Learnish is **community / word-of-mouth led**, not ad-led. The engine that every phase repeats and scales:

```
Weekend workshop  →  parents meet, kids make friends, local trust + proof
        ↓
Free family signup  ← north-star
        ↓
Digital Club  (cheap, all-digital, ~100% margin)
        ↓
Full Club  (kits + workshops, the anchor offer)
        ↓
Referrals + ambassadors  →  the next workshop
```

In-person sessions make the "safe and trusted" promise tangible, which is the one thing global attention apps (YouTube, TikTok, Roblox) and global EdTech apps (Khan Academy Kids, Duolingo) structurally cannot do here. See [operating principles](../strategy/operating-principles.md).

## How to use this playbook

Each phase file answers the same five questions:

1. **Goal** — what "done" looks like at the end of this phase.
2. **ICP focus** — which slice of families we're targeting *in this phase only*.
3. **Motion** — how we acquire and activate (founder-led, ambassador-led, referral, paid).
4. **Channels & content** — what we run and what we publish.
5. **Exit criteria** — the metric and qualitative signals that say "move to the next phase."

The marketing-skill folders at the repo root (`brand-voice/`, `campaigns/`, `competitive-analysis/`, etc.) are the *workshops* — playbook phases pull from them and push back into them.

## Milestone model

Phases are gated by **paying Club families** (the committed, referenceable core), while **free family signups** (north-star) scale faster in the background.

| # | Phase | File | Paying-family gate | One-line goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Foundation | [phase-0-foundation.md](phase-0-foundation.md) | — | Get positioning, the parent ICP, and the Club offer sharp enough to sell. |
| 1 | First 10 families — Founding cohort | [phase-1-first-10.md](phase-1-first-10.md) | ~10 Full Club families | Run lighthouse workshops in Tashkent and convert attending parents into a founding cohort that co-builds and refers. |
| 2 | First 100 families — Early adopters | [phase-2-first-100.md](phase-2-first-100.md) | ~100 paying families | Prove the workshop→signup→Club flywheel repeats across venues without the founder running each one. |
| 3 | First 1,000 families — Scale | [phase-3-first-1000.md](phase-3-first-1000.md) | ~1,000 paying families | Industrialize the workshop network across Tashkent districts and regional cities. |

> Beyond Phase 3, the playbook restarts at a new milestone (**10,000 families**) or pivots shape (new market, new product line).

## Top-level unknowns

Things that affect every phase below and need to be decided once:

- [unknown] **Willingness to pay.** Are the Club price points (~$5–8/mo Digital; ~$18 / $40 / $60 Full Club) right? (Validate in Phase 4 — Van Westendorp.)
- [unknown] **Founder bandwidth.** How much of the early motion (lighthouse workshops, founding-family relationships) is founder-led vs. delegated to ambassadors?
- [unknown] **Capital envelope.** What's the marketing budget per phase, and does it fund kit float / venue costs or pure acquisition?
- [unknown] **Ambassador supply.** Can we recruit enough student ambassadors + local teachers to keep workshop cadence ahead of demand?
- [unknown] **Language posture by channel.** Default working assumption: Uzbek + Russian in parallel; confirm which leads first-touch in each city.

## Review loop into this playbook

- Every weekly and monthly decision that changes assumptions must be recorded in [open-questions-register.md](open-questions-register.md).
- At the end of each month, update phase-level assumptions in the relevant phase doc.
- At the end of each quarter, adjust phase exit criteria only with explicit rationale.
