# Phase 2 — First 100 Families (Early Adopters)

> Prove the workshop→signup→Club flywheel repeats across venues and neighborhoods without the founder running every session.

## Goal

~90 more paying families on top of the Phase 1 founding cohort, acquired through a *repeatable* workshop + referral motion rather than the founder's personal relationships. The point isn't volume — it's *evidence that a student ambassador and a local teacher can fill a workshop and convert families without the founder in the room*.

In the background, free family signups (north-star) should be growing several times faster than the paying count, fed by workshops, referrals, and the standalone Digital Club funnel.

## ICP focus

Expand from one neighborhood to ~3–5 communities across Tashkent (different districts, a couple of schools, a kids-club partner). Same parent ICP, new local nodes.

## Motion

Hybrid, ambassador-led. **Student ambassadors** run on-the-ground workshop ops (sourcing local teachers, filling sessions, signups at the door), comped with benefits — membership, store credits, experience — not payroll. The founder takes only the strategic relationships (venue partners, the first regional node) and the public-facing storytelling. Referrals from founding families become a real source for the first time.

## Marketing artifacts to produce

- 3–5 family stories / testimonials from the founding cohort (with consent).
- Workshop-in-a-box playbook — so any ambassador can run a session to the same standard.
- Referral mechanic v1 (what a referring family earns — store credits / free month) — see [`../../pricing/monetization-model.md`](../../pricing/monetization-model.md).
- Live performance dashboard v1 (signups, workshop fill rate, free→paid conversion) — see [`../../performance-analytics/`](../../performance-analytics/).
- Editorial calendar with a weekly publishing rhythm tied to the workshop schedule.

## Exit criteria

Move to Phase 3 when:

- ~100 paying families on the Club.
- ≥ 50% of new paying families in the last 30 days came through a workshop or referral that the founder did **not** personally run.
- We have ≥ 2 repeatable sources (e.g. workshops + referrals) with a known cost per signup and free→paid conversion rate.
- Free→Digital→Full Club conversion rates are known and stable enough to forecast.
