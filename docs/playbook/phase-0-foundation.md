# Phase 0 — Foundation

> Get the positioning, the parent ICP, and the Club offer sharp enough that the first workshop and the first signup conversation both land.

This phase is mostly *writing things down* — there's no family target yet, just artifacts that the next three phases depend on.

## Goal

By the end of Phase 0:

- We can describe in one sentence what Learnish offers, to whom, and instead of what.
- We can name the first ICP slice for Phase 1 — specific enough that a list of 30 candidate families (a neighborhood, a school WhatsApp/Telegram group, a kids-club mailing list) is obvious.
- We have a working Club offer (tiers, price, what's included, what's not) we're willing to test against the first founding families — see [`../../pricing/monetization-model.md`](../../pricing/monetization-model.md).

## Inputs we need

- Founder/team alignment on the company thesis (captured in the [migration tracker](../migration-tracker.md) Phase 0 brief).
- The monetization model (Club tiers, workshop economics) — done, see [`../../pricing/monetization-model.md`](../../pricing/monetization-model.md).
- [unknown] Parent interviews to date — how many, what we heard about screen time, safety, and what they'd pay for.
- View on the competitor space, split **attention** (YouTube/TikTok/Roblox/Netflix) vs **category** (Khan Academy Kids, Duolingo, KiwiCo/MEL Science, Outschool, local kids clubs) — see [`../../competitive-analysis/`](../../competitive-analysis/).

## Exit criteria

Move to Phase 1 when:

- Positioning has been read aloud to ≥ 5 prospective parents without confusion.
- A named list of ≥ 20 candidate founding families exists, plus ≥ 1 confirmed workshop venue/date.
- We have a written Club offer (tiers, price, term) and a first-workshop offer ready to test.
- The "safe & trusted" claim has at least one concrete proof we can show parents (vetted teachers, content review, refund/guarantee, etc.).
