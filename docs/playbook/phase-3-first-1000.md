# Phase 3 — First 1,000 Families (Scale)

> Industrialize the workshop network across Tashkent districts and regional cities, so family acquisition runs on a budget-and-targets cadence rather than personal hustle.

## Goal

~900 more paying families on top of Phase 2, through a workshop network + referral + paid + content mix that operates predictably. By the end, Learnish has a marketing and community organization — a repeatable "node" model — not a founder with a Telegram account.

Free family signups (north-star) scale alongside, with the standalone app + Digital Club funnel now contributing meaningfully on its own.

## ICP focus

Multi-city. All of Tashkent's districts plus the first regional cities (regions run lower workshop volume and price by design — they seed local community nodes; see [`../../pricing/monetization-model.md`](../../pricing/monetization-model.md)). Same parent ICP, replicated by geography.

## Motion

Network-led. A community/marketing function owns demand (workshop calendar, ambassador network, content, paid). The product owns activation and retention in-app. The founder's role shifts to category and partnership work — the "make screen time exciting and safe" narrative, school/brand partnerships, regional expansion — not individual workshops.

## Marketing artifacts to produce

- Quarterly campaign briefs per channel and per region.
- Localized app, courses, and content in the priority language matrix (UZ/RU) — see [`../../brand-voice/`](../../brand-voice/).
- Refreshed positioning if the competitive landscape (attention apps, new local entrants) has shifted.
- Public milestone moments (the "we hit 500 families", "we hit 1,000 families" beats) to fuel word-of-mouth.
- Mature performance reporting — weekly, monthly, quarterly cadence with cohort retention.

## Exit criteria

This phase ends when ~1,000 paying families are on the Club and the workshop-node model is documented well enough that a new city can be opened from a playbook. After that, the playbook restarts at a new milestone (**10,000 families**) or pivots shape (new market, new product line, deeper commerce).
