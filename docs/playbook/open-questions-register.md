## Status Definitions

- **Open:** Decision needed, no final owner decision yet.
- **In progress:** Option analysis is underway.
- **Decided:** Final decision logged and active.
- **Deferred:** Explicitly postponed with review date.

## Update Rules

- Review this register weekly in Growth Ops.
- No phase or monthly plan changes without updating this register.
- Close each item with date, approver, and rationale; move it to the Decided table.
