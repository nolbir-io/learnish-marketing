# Operating Cadence

Operating rhythm for Learnish marketing and community over the next 6 months.

## Weekly Growth Ops (60 min)

- **Owner:** Marketing lead
- **Participants:** Community/workshop owner, content owner, analytics owner, product/activation representative
- **Inputs:** `performance-analytics/weekly-report-template.md`, workshop calendar, campaign calendar, blocker list
- **Agenda:**
  1. Review last week's KPI deltas — family signups (north-star), workshop fill rate, free→paid conversion — and top/underperforming channels.
  2. Confirm this week's workshops and launches and their dependencies (venues, ambassadors, kits).
  3. Resolve blockers and assign owners with due dates.
- **Outputs:** Weekly commitments, risk updates, changes to experiment priorities.

## Weekly Brand and Content Review (45 min)

- **Owner:** Brand lead
- **Participants:** Content lead, language reviewer (UZ/RU), community/workshop owner
- **Inputs:** Draft queue from `content-creation/`, rubric from `brand-review/review-rubric.md`
- **Agenda:**
  1. Review highest-impact in-flight assets, including real-workshop photos/stories (check consent).
  2. Approve, request changes, or reject with clear rationale — held to the "safe, trusted, kid-appropriate" bar.
  3. Track systemic issues to update voice/language rules.
- **Outputs:** Approved ship list, feedback log entries, rule updates if needed.

## Biweekly Experiment Review (45 min)

- **Owner:** Growth/analytics owner
- **Participants:** Marketing lead, community/workshop owner, product/activation representative
- **Inputs:** `performance-analytics/experiments-log.md`
- **Agenda:**
  1. Evaluate active experiments against success criteria (workshop formats, referral incentives, Digital Club funnel, onboarding).
  2. Decide one action per test: scale, iterate, or stop.
  3. Prioritize next experiment backlog.
- **Outputs:** Decision per experiment and updated priorities.

## Monthly Business Review (90 min)

- **Owner:** Marketing lead
- **Participants:** Founders, marketing core team, community/workshop lead, product/activation lead
- **Inputs:** Monthly KPI rollup, spend summary, workshop P&L, campaign outcomes
- **Agenda:**
  1. Compare KPI performance against the month target in `docs/plan-6m/`.
  2. Review cost per family, free→paid conversion, workshop economics, and channel mix.
  3. Confirm what to double down on next month.
- **Outputs:** Month plan adjustments and explicit leadership decisions.

## Quarterly GTM Checkpoint (Half-day)

- **Owner:** Founding team
- **Participants:** Cross-functional GTM + community leaders
- **Inputs:** Playbook phases and prior quarter performance
- **Agenda:**
  1. Reassess parent ICP and Club offer assumptions.
  2. Confirm phase exit criteria realism (paying-family gates).
  3. Rebalance budget and team/ambassador capacity; review regional expansion readiness.
- **Outputs:** Updated phase priorities and strategic change log.

## Meeting Hygiene Rules

- Every recurring meeting has a single owner and a pre-read posted 24h before.
- Every decision records owner, date, and rationale in `docs/playbook/open-questions-register.md`.
- Unowned actions are invalid and must be assigned before meeting close.
