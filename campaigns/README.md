# campaigns

The phased campaign plan that grows Learnish from the first lighthouse workshops to **1,000 paying Club families** — built on the workshop flywheel.

## Purpose

Hold one brief per campaign — the campaign objective, the playbook phase it serves (first 10 → first 100 → first 1,000 families), the channels, the budget, the owner, and the success metrics. Pulls voice from [`brand-voice/`](../brand-voice/), copy from [`content-creation/`](../content-creation/), pricing from [`pricing/monetization-model.md`](../pricing/monetization-model.md), and reports back to [`performance-analytics/`](../performance-analytics/).

## The engine: the workshop flywheel

Every Learnish campaign turns the same loop. We don't run ad-led acquisition; we run a community/word-of-mouth motion anchored on in-person trust.

`workshop → free family signup → Digital Club → Full Club → referral → next workshop`

- **North-star (scales in the background):** free family account signups (`learnish.uz`).
- **Phase gate (the committed core):** paying Club families — **10 → 100 → 1,000**.
- **The hook is tangible:** STEAM toys + weekend workshops. The Learnish Club is the wrapper introduced *after* the workshop earns trust (per [voice guide](../brand-voice/voice-guide.md)).

## Key questions each brief answers

- What's the milestone, and what does the workshop flywheel need to do to clear it (founder-led → ambassador-led → network-led)?
- What does this phase require — a new district, a new ambassador, a venue partner, a referral mechanic, a paid top-up?
- Which motions are compounding (referrals, SEO, community) vs. time-bound pushes (a workshop wave, a partner co-promo)?
- How does family acquisition hand off to activation/retention (marketing stops, product + community begin)?
- What does a cost-per-signup / cost-per-family ceiling look like at this stage?
