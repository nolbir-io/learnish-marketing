## Objective

**Industrialize the workshop network** across Tashkent districts and the first regional cities, so family acquisition runs on a budget-and-targets cadence rather than personal hustle. Add ~900 more paying Club families on top of Phase 2 through a workshop network + referral + paid + content mix that operates predictably.

By the end, Learnish has a marketing and community *organization* — a repeatable "node" model — not a founder with a Telegram account.

## North-star & gate

- **North-star:** free family account signups — now with the standalone app + Digital Club funnel contributing meaningfully on its own.
- **Phase gate:** **~1,000 paying Club families.**
- **Engine:** the workshop flywheel, franchised by geography — each node = a district/city + venue + ambassador + local teachers.

## ICP focus (this phase)

- **Multi-city.** All of Tashkent's districts plus the first regional cities (regions run lower workshop volume and price by design — they seed local community nodes; see [monetization model](../pricing/monetization-model.md)).
- Same parent ICP, replicated by geography.
- Self-serve app signup (no workshop) may become a primary motion at scale — to be validated.

## Core message

**To parents:** the proven version of the Phase 1/2 message, now backed by real numbers and milestones — "the weekend program thousands of families across Uzbekistan trust." Lead safe & trusted (reviewed, age-appropriate content); hero the STEAM toys + workshops; Club is the wrapper.

**Category narrative (founder-led):** "Make screen time exciting and safe" — the anti-YouTube/attention wedge plus the **local in-person trust** that global attention apps and EdTech apps can't replicate here (per [Phase 5 competitive intelligence](../competitive-analysis/market-map.md)).

## Motion

**Network-led.** A community/marketing function owns demand (workshop calendar, ambassador network, content, paid). The product owns activation and retention in-app. The founder shifts to category and partnership work — the screen-time narrative, school/brand partnerships, regional expansion — not individual workshops.

## Marketing artifacts to produce

- Quarterly campaign briefs per channel and per region.
- Localized app, courses, and content in the priority language matrix (UZ/RU) — see [`brand-voice/`](../brand-voice/).
- Refreshed positioning if the competitive landscape (attention apps, new local entrants) has shifted.
- Public milestone moments ("we hit 500 families", "we hit 1,000 families") to fuel word-of-mouth.
- Mature performance reporting — weekly/monthly/quarterly cadence with cohort retention.

## Budget & resources

- **Portfolio-style allocation by expected cost-per-family and payback**, reinvesting workshop/Club revenue back into the node network.
- Dedicated owners per major channel (workshop network, referrals, paid, content/SEO).
- Paid digital becomes a real (still-tunable) line rather than a small local top-up — but the workshop network stays the trust engine, not paid.

## Success metrics

- **~1,000 paying Club families** (the phase gate).
- **Multi-source resilience** — no single-channel dependence; ≥ 3 acquisition sources with known unit economics.
- **Positive payback trend** and stable activation/retention quality (cohort retention holding).
- Free family signups scaling alongside, with the Digital Club funnel contributing on its own.

## Exit criteria

- [ ] ~1,000 paying Club families.
- [ ] The workshop-node model documented well enough that **a new city can be opened from a playbook**.
- [ ] ≥ 3 resilient acquisition sources with known cost-per-family and payback.
- [ ] Cohort retention stable enough to forecast LTV.

After this, the playbook restarts at a new milestone (**10,000 families**) or changes shape (new market, new product line, deeper commerce).

## Risks & open questions

- **Budget envelope** for paid channels vs. reinvesting in the workshop network.
- **Org shape** — size of the community + marketing team, and the ambassador-to-city ratio.
- **Self-serve at scale** — does no-workshop app signup become a primary motion?
- **Regional sequence** — which cities after Tashkent, and Central Asia beyond Uzbekistan.
- **Pricing/packaging at scale** — same Club tiers or new ones.
