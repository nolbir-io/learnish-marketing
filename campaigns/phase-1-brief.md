## Objective

Run lighthouse weekend workshops in Tashkent and convert attending parents into the **first 10 trusted families** — a small, committed core that joins the Learnish Club, co-builds the offer, and refers the next families. This phase proves the workshop → free signup → Club flywheel works at least once, founder-led, before we try to repeat it.

**Why "trusted families," not just "customers":** these 10 are chosen for relationship and word-of-mouth reach, not revenue. They are the proof, the feedback loop, and the first referral engine. Treat them like co-founders of the community.

## North-star & gate

- **North-star (scales in the background):** free family account signups.
- **Phase gate (the committed core):** **~10 paying Full Club families.**
- **Engine:** the workshop flywheel — `workshop → free signup → Digital Club → Full Club → referral → next workshop`.

## ICP focus (this phase only)

- **Primary buyer:** growth-minded parents of kids aged 5–17 in **Tashkent** (start with 1–2 districts near the first venues) who feel the screen-time tension and will show up in person.
- **Choose for trust + reach:** parents who are connected locally (school parent groups, neighbors, community organizers) and willing to give honest feedback and refer if they love it.
- **End users:** their kids 5–17 — the experience must delight them or parents won't renew.
- **Deliberately out of scope this phase:** regional cities, fully-remote-only families (app/courses not shipped), and anyone who only wants free passive entertainment (anti-persona).

## Core message

**To parents (trust first):** "A safe, exciting Saturday for your child — vetted teachers, reviewed age-appropriate projects, and other parents in the room. Your first workshop is free." `[PROOF — confirm free-first-workshop offer]`

**To kids (delight):** "Come build something real this weekend." (Nish, the Learnish bee, carries the kid-facing invite.)

**The hook is the tangible offer** — STEAM workshops (and toys) — not the abstract membership. The Learnish Club is introduced *after* the workshop earns trust. Lead safety with *reviewed, age-appropriate content* (per voice guide).

## Motion

**Founder-led and high-touch.** This phase is about relationships, not scale:

- Founder + first ambassador(s) personally run the lighthouse workshops and meet every parent.
- Direct, warm invitations (Telegram, school parent groups, neighbors, in-person) — not paid acquisition at scale.
- After each workshop: personal follow-up, free family account, then a no-pressure Club conversation with families who clearly loved it.
- Capture feedback and verbatim parent/kid language at every session (feeds `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` "Customer Language" — currently to-collect).

## Offer

- **First workshop free** `[PROOF — confirm]` — the low-pressure entry that makes "safe and trusted" tangible.
- **Free family account** — the north-star action, captured at or after every session (`learnish.uz`).
- **Full Club for the trusted core** — kits + workshops + courses, intro pricing **being validated** (Phase 4 WTP); annual prepay is the focus. Don't present unvalidated prices as final (`pricing/monetization-model.md`).

## Budget & resources

- **Low paid spend; high founder/relationship effort.** This is a community motion, not an ad motion.
- Costs concentrate in **workshop delivery** (teacher 30% + venue 30% per the marketplace split) and **kit float** for the first Full Club families — not media buying.
- A small **local social budget** is optional to fill the first sessions (see `content-creation/paid-ads/`); keep it minimal and geo-targeted to Tashkent parents.
- Resource focus: a repeatable workshop playbook, the landing page, Telegram comms, and on-the-ground ambassador ops.

## Success metrics

- **~10 paying Full Club families** (the phase gate).
- **Free family signups** from workshop attendees (north-star; track signup rate per session).
- **Workshop fill rate** and **repeat-attendance** (did families come back?).
- **Workshop → free signup → Club** conversion at each step (first read of the flywheel).
- **Referrals:** at least some of the 10 arrive via another family's referral (proof the loop turns).
- **Qualitative:** ≥10 documented parent/kid quotes; a referenceable founding cohort willing to be named `[with consent]`.

## Exit criteria (move to Phase 2)

- [ ] ~10 paying Full Club families, at least a few referral-sourced.
- [ ] The workshop → signup → Club flywheel has completed end-to-end at least once, repeatably.
- [ ] A documented, repeatable workshop playbook so a session can run **without the founder** in the room (the Phase 2 test).
- [ ] Real proof collected: parent/kid quotes, session photos, and the first willingness-to-pay signal.

## Risks & open questions

- **Willingness to pay** for the Club price points — validate in Phase 4 (Van Westendorp). Until then, prices are intro/illustrative.
- **Founder bandwidth** — how much of the motion is founder-led vs. delegated to ambassadors (top-level playbook unknown).
- **Ambassador/teacher supply** — can we recruit enough vetted teachers + student ambassadors to keep cadence ahead of demand?
- **Free-first-workshop economics** — confirm the offer and its run-rate; each comped seat carries the teacher + venue payout.
- **Venue access** — secured via the venue/partner motion (local centers as partners, not just rivals — see Phase 5 decision).
