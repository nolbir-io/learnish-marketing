# Campaign Calendar

Quarter-by-quarter campaign schedule across the [6-month plan](../docs/plan-6m/README.md). Milestones gate on **paying Club families**; free family signups (north-star) scale in the background. The engine every month is the workshop flywheel: `workshop → free signup → Digital Club → Full Club → referral → next workshop`.

## Q1 (Months 1–3) — Phase 1: First 10 trusted families

| Month | Focus | Key campaigns | Owner | KPI focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Lighthouse setup | Secure first venue(s), recruit first ambassador/teacher, stand up workshop landing page + Telegram | Founder | Venue + teacher secured; landing page live |
| M2 | First workshops | Founder-led weekend workshops; warm Telegram/school-group invites; signups at the door | Founder | Workshop fill rate; free signups per session |
| M3 | Convert the core | Post-workshop follow-up → free account → no-pressure Club conversation; collect quotes/photos | Founder | ~10 paying Club families; first referrals |

## Q2 (Months 4–6) — Phase 2: First 100 families (early adopters)

| Month | Focus | Key campaigns | Owner | KPI focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M4 | Make it repeatable | Workshop-in-a-box playbook; first ambassador-run sessions in a 2nd/3rd district | Campaign Owner | % families from non-founder-run sessions |
| M5 | Turn on referrals | Referral mechanic v1 (store credits / free month); founding-family stories; Digital Club funnel test | Marketing Lead | ≥2 sources w/ known cost per signup |
| M6 | Systematize + plan scale | Multi-venue cadence, partner co-marketing (schools/kids clubs), Phase 3 + next-cycle brief | Marketing Lead | ~100 paying Club families; free→paid conversion |

## Beyond M6 — Phase 3: First 1,000 families (scale)

Workshop network across Tashkent districts + first regional cities; referral + paid + SEO/content mix on a budget-and-targets cadence. Build a per-region/per-channel quarterly brief cadence (see [`phase-3-brief.md`](phase-3-brief.md)).

## Always-on (all phases)

- **Telegram** parent comms + per-district community groups.
- **Instagram** real session proof (reels/photos, founding-family stories, with consent).
- **Referrals** — "bring a friend" at every session; ask happy families.
- **learnish.uz** free family account + STEAM store.

## Update rule

Update campaign dates, owners, and statuses **weekly** during Growth Ops. Log scale/iterate/stop decisions in [`performance-analytics/experiments-log.md`](../performance-analytics/experiments-log.md) and channel/CAC decisions in [`docs/playbook/open-questions-register.md`](../docs/playbook/open-questions-register.md).
