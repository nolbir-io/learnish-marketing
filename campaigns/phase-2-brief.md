## Objective

Prove the **workshop → free signup → Club** flywheel *repeats* across venues and neighborhoods **without the founder running every session**. Get to ~100 paying Club families on top of the Phase 1 founding cohort — acquired through a repeatable, ambassador-led workshop + referral motion, not the founder's personal relationships.

**The point isn't volume — it's evidence** that a student ambassador and a vetted local teacher can fill a workshop and convert families on their own.

## North-star & gate

- **North-star (scales in the background):** free family account signups — should grow several times faster than the paying count, fed by workshops, referrals, and the standalone Digital Club funnel.
- **Phase gate (the committed core):** **~100 paying Club families.**
- **Engine:** the workshop flywheel, now franchised — `workshop → free signup → Digital Club → Full Club → referral → next workshop`.

## ICP focus (this phase)

- Same parent ICP as Phase 1 — growth-minded parents of kids 5–17 who feel the screen-time tension — replicated across **~3–5 communities** in Tashkent (different districts, a couple of schools, a kids-club partner).
- New local **nodes**, not a new audience: each node is a district + a venue + an ambassador.
- End users stay the kids 5–17 — the session has to delight them or parents won't renew.
- **Out of scope this phase:** regional cities (Phase 3), fully-remote-only families (app/courses still rolling out), passive-entertainment-only parents (anti-persona).

## Core message

**To parents (trust first):** "The weekend program other parents in your district already trust — vetted teachers, reviewed age-appropriate projects, and real things your child builds. Your first workshop is free." `[PROOF — collect founding-family quotes + session photos]`

**To kids (delight):** "Come build something real this weekend — bring a friend." (Nish, the Learnish bee, carries the kid-facing invite.)

Lead with **safe & trusted = reviewed, age-appropriate content**; the hook is the tangible STEAM toys + workshops, with the Club introduced after the session earns trust (per [voice guide](../brand-voice/voice-guide.md)). New wedge available now: **real founding-family proof** ("families in [district] already do this").

## Motion

**Hybrid, ambassador-led.** Student ambassadors run on-the-ground workshop ops — sourcing vetted local teachers, filling sessions, signups at the door — comped with benefits (membership, store credits, experience), not payroll. The founder takes only the strategic relationships (venue partners, the first regional node) and public storytelling. **Referrals from founding families become a real acquisition source for the first time.**

## Offer

- **First workshop free** `[PROOF — confirm]` — the low-pressure entry that makes "safe and trusted" tangible in each new district.
- **Free family account** — the north-star action, captured at every session (`learnish.uz`).
- **Club tiers** — Digital Club (standalone, low-price entry) and Full Club (kits + workshops + courses), **intro pricing being validated** (Phase 4 WTP); annual prepay is the focus. Don't present unvalidated prices as final.
- **Referral reward** — what a referring family earns; finalize the mechanic in [`pricing/monetization-model.md`](../pricing/monetization-model.md).

## Budget & resources

- **Still low paid spend; effort shifts from founder to ambassador ops.** Community motion, not an ad motion.
- Costs concentrate in **workshop delivery** (teacher 30% + venue 30% marketplace split), **kit float** for new Full Club families, and **ambassador benefits**.
- A small **local social budget** (Instagram/Telegram, geo-targeted to Tashkent districts) can fill new-venue sessions — keep it minimal; see [`content-creation/paid-ads/`](../content-creation/paid-ads/).
- Resource focus: the workshop-in-a-box playbook, ambassador recruiting/retention, referral mechanic, and a live performance dashboard v1.

## Success metrics

- **~100 paying Club families** (the phase gate).
- **≥ 50%** of new paying families in the last 30 days came through a workshop or referral the founder did **not** personally run.
- **≥ 2 repeatable sources** (e.g. workshops + referrals) with a known cost per signup and free→paid conversion rate.
- **Free → Digital → Full Club** conversion rates known and stable enough to forecast.
- Free family signups growing faster than the paying count (north-star healthy).

## Exit criteria (move to Phase 3)

- [ ] ~100 paying Club families.
- [ ] ≥ 50% of recent paying families from non-founder-run workshops/referrals.
- [ ] ≥ 2 repeatable acquisition sources with known unit economics.
- [ ] Workshop-in-a-box playbook proven across ≥ 3 venues by different ambassadors.
- [ ] Free→Digital→Full Club conversion known and stable.

## Risks & open questions

- **Ambassador recruiting & retention** — how many can run at once, and what comp keeps them (top playbook unknown).
- **CAC ceiling per family** at this stage (workshop cost ÷ families acquired).
- **Does the Digital Club convert standalone**, or only as a workshop follow-up?
- **Referral timing** — how hard to push referrals before the experience is consistent enough to be worth referring.
- **Willingness to pay** for the Club price points — validate in Phase 4 (Van Westendorp); prices stay intro/illustrative until then.
