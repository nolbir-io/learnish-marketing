# competitive-brief

One-page distillations of [`competitive-analysis/`](../competitive-analysis/) — the version a founder, marketer, or workshop ambassador reads in 60 seconds before talking to a parent.

> **Status:** Phase 5 (Competitive intelligence) of the [migration](../docs/migration-tracker.md), rewritten for Learnish.

## Purpose

Turn research into operational ammunition. Each brief answers: *when does Learnish win against this alternative, when does it lose, and what do we say to a parent?*

Because Learnish sells to **parents** about their **kids**, briefs are framed for the parent conversation (and the workshop pitch), not a B2B sales call.

## Key questions per competitor

- Who uses it — the child (attention) or the parent who pays (category)?
- What's its price and one-sentence pitch?
- When does Learnish win head-to-head? When does it lose?
- What's the **trap question** — the thing we want a parent to ask the competitor that exposes its weakness?
- Compete or **partner**? (e.g. local tutoring centers can become workshop venues.)

## What goes here

- [`template.md`](template.md) — the one-page battlecard format
- `<competitor>.md` — one brief per priority competitor (distilled from its [profile](../competitor-profiles/))
