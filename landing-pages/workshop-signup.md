# Landing page — Workshop signup (English source draft)

**Page purpose:** Convert local Tashkent parents into a booked seat at a Learnish weekend workshop — the top of the community-led flywheel (`docs/playbook/README.md`). The workshop is the tangible, trust-building first touch; the free family account and the Learnish Club follow.
**Primary CTA:** Book a weekend workshop seat
**Secondary CTA:** Create a free family account (anchor link / lightweight form)
**Audience:**
- **Primary:** Parents of kids aged 5–17 in Tashkent who feel the daily tension between passive screen time and real growth, and want a safe, trusted, exciting thing for their child to do this weekend.
- **Secondary:** Parents already curious about Learnish (referred by another family or an ambassador) who are comparing it to scattered apps, toys, and tutoring centers.
**Traffic assumption:** Mostly warm + local — word-of-mouth referrals, ambassador shares, and geo-targeted local social. Low-pressure intent: "what is this Saturday thing the other parents mentioned?"
**Positioning:** Learnish makes everyday learning **exciting** for kids 5–17 — and a **weekend workshop** is the easiest way to see it for yourself. Safe and trusted (reviewed, age-appropriate content; vetted teachers; you're in the room), genuinely fun for the kid, and the on-ramp to the Learnish Club.
**Source-of-truth context:** `.agents/product-marketing-context.md`, `brand-voice/voice-guide.md`, `brand-voice/language-matrix.md`, `pricing/monetization-model.md`, `competitor-profiles/_summary.md`
**Translation:** **Uzbek is the lead** for parent first-touch; produce English + Russian alongside per `brand-voice/language-matrix.md`. Mirror the headline, the safety promise, and the CTA in all three — no English-only buttons on an Uzbek page. Hyper-local workshop flyers lead in the district's language.
**Site / store:** `learnish.uz` (STEAM toys store + family account live there).

> **Proof gating:** Everything marked `[PROOF — to collect]` (workshop topics, dates, venues, prices, teacher names, parent quotes, photos) must be replaced with **real** details before publish. Per the copywriting and brand-voice rules, fabricated stats, testimonials, or outcomes are out of bounds. Default to honest placeholders or remove the section if the proof isn't ready. The app/courses are **not shipped** — do not promise "download the app" as a live action; lead with the workshop (live) and the free family account.

> **Voice gates (from `brand-voice/voice-guide.md`):** Two audiences at once — trust for the parent, delight for the kid; when they conflict, **trust wins**. Lead safety with *reviewed, age-appropriate content*. Messaging hero = *STEAM toys + weekend workshops*; the Club is the wrapper. Mascot **Nish** (the Learnish bee) is **kid-facing only** — keep Nish out of parent trust/pricing copy. No hype words (revolutionary, best-in-class, guaranteed results) and no merchant/e-commerce holdovers.

## 2. The tension (problem section)

**Section header:**
You don't want another app. You want a weekend that's actually good for them.

**Body:**
Every parent knows the feeling. The tablet goes on "just for a bit," and an hour later it's still on. You want your child learning, making, moving — not scrolling. But the alternatives are scattered and hard to trust:

- **Apps win the time but don't grow the child.** YouTube, TikTok, Roblox — designed to keep your child watching, not building.
- **Vetting takes work you don't have time for.** Is this course any good? Is this content safe? Is this tutor worth it? Every option is a separate decision.
- **Everything is in a different place.** One app for languages, a toy store across town, a tutoring center on the other side, a STEAM kit you order from abroad and wait weeks for.
- **None of it builds a community.** Your child learns alone, on a screen, with no friends and no shared Saturday to look forward to.

**Closing line:**
Learnish puts the good stuff in one place — and starts with something you can see with your own eyes: a weekend workshop.

**Why this section:** Names the screen-time tension *gently* (voice guide: "the tension, named gently") without shaming the parent, then reframes scattered alternatives — the exact pains in `.agents/product-marketing-context.md`. Sets up the workshop as the concrete answer.
